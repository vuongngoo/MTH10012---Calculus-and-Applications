# 1. Useful Formulas
### Radians to Degrees

$$
\text{Radians} \times \frac{180}{\pi} = \text{Degrees}
$$

$$
\text{Degrees} \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \text{Radians}
$$

### Logarithmic Laws

$$
\log_a a = 1
$$

$$
\log_a 1 = 0
$$

$$
\log_a mn = \log_a m + \log_a n
$$

$$
\log_a m^p = p\log_a m
$$

$$
\log_a \frac{m}{n} = \log_a m - \log_a n
$$

$$
\log_a \frac{1}{n} = -\log_a n
$$

$$
a^{\log_a m} = m
$$

$$
\log_a 0 \text{ cannot exist}
$$

$$
\log_a(\text{negative}) \text{ cannot exist}
$$

$$
\log_a m = \log_a n \implies m = n
$$

$$
\log_a b = \frac{\log_c b}{\log_c a}
$$

$$
\log_a m = n \iff a^n = m
$$

### Index Laws

$$
a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}
$$

$$
\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}
$$

$$
a^0 = 1
$$

$$
a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}
$$

$$
(a^m)^n = a^{m\times n}
$$

$$
(ab)^n = a^n \times b^n
$$

$$
a^{\frac{1}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a}
$$

$$
a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m
$$

$$
a^b = a^c \implies b = c
$$
